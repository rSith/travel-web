# 🌴 Ambalangoda Website

A website about **Ambalangoda**, a beautiful coastal town in the Galle District of Sri Lanka's Southern Province. It is famous for its traditional **mask carving**, cultural shows, and lovely beaches.

---

## 📖 About the Project

This website helps visitors learn about Ambalangoda: its culture, its famous places, and the things to do there. It was built as a **group project** by our team.

**Who is it for?**
- Tourists who plan to visit Ambalangoda
- Students who want to learn about local culture
- Anyone who loves Sri Lankan traditions

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Home page** with a welcome section and photos of the town
- 🎭 **Culture page** about traditional masks and folk dance
- 📍 **Places to visit** such as the mask museum and the beach
- 🖼️ **Photo gallery**
- 📞 **Contact page** to send us a message
- 📱 **Mobile friendly** design that works on phones and laptops
